The Story

Born in the Hour Before Dawn

The Midnight Collection is not about darkness for its own sake. It is about the clarity that only exists when everything else has fallen away.

Every piece is constructed from heavyweight combed cotton — minimum 300 GSM — chosen not for softness, but for structure. These are garments that hold their shape. That hold their ground. That age with the man who wears them.

The silhouettes are oversized, but never careless. Every proportion is considered. Every seam is a decision.
